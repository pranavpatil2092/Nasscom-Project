# 🌍 Global AI Adoption in Education

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **AI adoption in the education sector** across different countries and regions using **Tableau**.

The project focuses on students, teachers, schools, AI tools, rural vs. urban usage, and AI adoption trends over time.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Education stakeholders need a simple and interactive way to understand global AI adoption, compare regions, identify rural-urban gaps, and analyze student and teacher AI usage.

## 📊 Dataset

* **Dataset:** Global AI Adoption in Education
* **Format:** CSV
* **Key fields:** Year, Country, Region, Student AI Usage, Teacher AI Usage, School AI Adoption, Average Daily AI Usage, Top AI Tool, Internet Penetration, Rural/Urban AI Usage, etc.

## ❓ Business Questions

* What is the overall level of AI adoption?
* Which countries have higher AI usage?
* How does student usage compare with teacher usage?
* Which AI tools are most commonly used?
* How has AI usage changed over time?
* What is the difference between rural and urban AI usage?
* Which regions have higher school AI adoption?

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing

The dataset was checked for:

* Missing and blank values
* Duplicate records
* Inconsistent values
* Incorrect data types
* Unnecessary columns
* Inconsistent numerical formats

The cleaned data was then prepared and connected to Tableau.

## 📈 Dashboards

### Dashboard 1

Provides an overall view of:

* AI adoption KPIs
* Country-wise AI adoption
* Regional adoption
* Rural vs. urban usage
* AI adoption levels

### Dashboard 2

Focuses on:

* Student vs. teacher AI usage
* Average daily AI usage
* Leading AI tools
* AI adoption trends

The dashboards use **KPIs, bar charts, line charts, maps, comparison charts and interactive filters**.

## 📖 Tableau Story

The story presents the analysis in a logical flow:

**Overall AI Adoption → Global Map → Student & Teacher Usage → Average Daily Usage → Leading AI Tools → Rural vs. Urban Growth → Regional Adoption → Year-wise Adoption**.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* AI adoption varies across countries and regions.
* Student and teacher AI usage can be compared across countries.
* AI tool usage differs between tools.
* Rural and urban AI adoption shows a noticeable gap.
* AI adoption changes over time.
* Regional school adoption varies.
